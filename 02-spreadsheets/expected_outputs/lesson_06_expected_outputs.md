## Exercise 5: Chart Building

There is not one exact image to match. A solid finished set should have:

- A column chart showing total revenue by region, sorted highest to lowest.

- Clear chart titles, readable data labels, and tidy formatting.
- No unnecessary legends, gridlines, or clutter that make the chart harder to read.
