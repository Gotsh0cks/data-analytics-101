## Exercise 1: Explore the Titanic Dataset

Expected objective answers:

```text
Shape: 891 rows x 12 columns
Survival percentage: 38.38%
Average age: 29.70
Columns with missing values: 3
Age: 177 missing
Cabin: 687 missing
Embarked: 2 missing
```

A good interpretation mentions that fewer than half of passengers in this dataset survived, the average age is about 30, and `Cabin` has a lot of missing data.

## Exercise 2: Tipping Patterns

Expected objective answers:

```text
Average tip: 3.00
Highest average tip day: Sun, 3.26
Dinner average tip: 3.10
Lunch average tip: 2.73
```

A good interpretation says that dinner parties tipped more than lunch parties on average in this dataset. It should also mention that the difference is based on averages, not every individual table.

## Exercise 3: Iris Species Comparison

Expected average petal length by species:

```text
setosa: 1.46
versicolor: 4.26
virginica: 5.55
```

Expected widest average sepal width:

```text
setosa: 3.43
```

A good interpretation compares the species in plain English. For example, it should notice that `setosa` has much shorter petals than the other two species, while also having the widest sepals on average.

## Exercise 4: Find Your Own Dataset

Because this exercise depends on the dataset you choose, there is no single numeric answer. Your work should include:

- Shape printed.
- Column names printed.
- Data types printed.
- Missing values checked.
- Three answerable questions written down.

Good questions should connect directly to columns in the dataset. A strong answer also includes one or two sentences explaining why those questions might be useful or interesting.
